## 🇻🇳 I. Jobs Related to Politics in Vietnam

Vietnam operates under a **one-party socialist system** led by the **Communist Party of Vietnam (CPV)**.
So, political careers often intertwine with **state agencies**, **mass organizations**, and **policy-oriented institutes** rather than partisan politics.

### 1. **Core Political & Government Roles**

| Job                                                                                        | Where You Work                                                                                      | Notes                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cán bộ / Công chức Nhà nước (Government Official)**                                      | Ministries (Bộ), People’s Committees (UBND), or Departments (Sở, Phòng)                             | Entry through civil service exams; involves policy enforcement, administration, and governance. |
| **Đại biểu Quốc hội / Hội đồng Nhân dân (National Assembly or People’s Council Delegate)** | Elected or appointed representatives                                                                | Involved in lawmaking, public consultation, and oversight.                                      |
| **Party Staff / Researcher (Cán bộ Đảng)**                                                 | CPV Central Office, provincial or district Party Committees                                         | Focused on political theory, organization, and party development.                               |
| **Chuyên viên Chính sách (Policy Officer / Analyst)**                                      | Ministries (Planning, Finance, Education, etc.) or Central Institute for Economic Management (CIEM) | Research and draft policy recommendations.                                                      |
| **Ngoại giao viên (Diplomat / Foreign Affairs Officer)**                                   | Ministry of Foreign Affairs, embassies, consulates                                                  | Represent Vietnam abroad.                                                                       |
| **Cán bộ Đoàn / Hội / Công đoàn (Mass Organization Officer)**                              | Youth Union (Đoàn), Women’s Union, Labor Union, Fatherland Front                                    | Public mobilization, social development programs.                                               |

### 2. **Research, Policy & Advisory**

| Role                                         | Example Institutions                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Policy Researcher / Think Tank Analyst       | Vietnam Academy of Social Sciences (VASS), Central Economic Commission |
| Economist or Statistician (Public Sector)    | General Statistics Office (GSO), Ministry of Planning and Investment   |
| Strategic Consultant for Government Projects | Ministries or local authorities via consulting firms                   |
| Public Policy Lecturer / Researcher          | National Academy of Politics, Fulbright University Vietnam             |

### 3. **Media & Political Communication**

| Role                           | Typical Place                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Political Journalist           | VTV, VOV, VietnamNet, Tuổi Trẻ, Thanh Niên                  |
| Press Officer (Cán bộ Báo chí) | Ministries, Party Committees, embassies                     |
| Government Spokesperson        | Offices of State agencies                                   |
| Public Relations Specialist    | State enterprises, local governments, or international NGOs |

### 4. **International & NGO-Related**

| Role                              | Organizations                                            |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Project Officer / Program Manager | UNDP, UNICEF, Oxfam, World Bank Vietnam                  |
| Development Policy Analyst        | International think tanks or donors                      |
| Advocacy Specialist               | NGOs focused on education, environment, governance, etc. |

## 🌟 II. KOL (Key Opinion Leader) Roles in Vietnam

Vietnam’s KOL scene is one of the fastest-growing in Southeast Asia — blending **entertainment, education, and social advocacy**.
KOLs can be **entertainers**, **experts**, or **public commentators** with large influence on **Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, or LinkedIn**.

### 1. **Entertainment / Lifestyle KOLs**

| Type                        | Example                            |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Celebrity, Singer, Actor    | Mỹ Tâm, Sơn Tùng M-TP              |
| Beauty & Fashion Influencer | Chloe Nguyễn, Trinh Phạm           |
| Travel & Lifestyle Blogger  | Khoai Lang Thang, Quang Linh Vlogs |
| Food Reviewer / TikToker    | Ninh Tito, Long Chun               |

### 2. **Professional / Expert KOLs**

| Field                | Example                                                                 |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tech & AI            | Ngô Đức Cảnh (AI educator), top AI/ML YouTubers                         |
| Business / Marketing | Đỗ Cao Bảo (FPT), Tuấn Hà (Vinalink)                                    |
| Education            | Giang Ơi, Đặng Hoàng Giang, Thạc sĩ / Tiến sĩ speaking on social issues |
| Finance / Economy    | Economists, investment YouTubers like Shark Linh, Shark Hưng            |

### 3. **Political / Social KOLs**

*(Note: “political influencers” in Vietnam must align with state regulations — open political campaigning or opposition content is restricted.)*

| Type                               | Role                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Civic / Youth Influencer**       | Speaks on national pride, volunteering, social development                                                                       |
| **Government Communication KOLs**  | Social-media figures collaborating with state media for awareness campaigns (anti-fraud, digital transformation, traffic safety) |
| **Academic or Policy Commentator** | Professors, analysts sharing insights on governance or economics (e.g., Dr. Lê Hồng Hiệp, Dr. Nguyễn Đức Thành)                  |
